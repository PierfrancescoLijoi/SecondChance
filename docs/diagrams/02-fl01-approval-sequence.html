From surplus offer to approved mission

FR-022

Humans create missions, the solver doesn't

  • The worker only writes ranked proposals with their reasons.
  • A mission exists only after POST /approve by an authorised coordinator.
  • A rejection keeps its reason in the audit trail.

FR-016 · NFR-006

Safe under concurrency and retries

  • Approve runs UPDATE offer … WHERE status = 'available' AND version = :v.
  • Zero rows updated → 409 Conflict, nothing else written.
  • Clients send an Idempotency-Key: a retried approve never creates a second mission.
  • Failed notifications land in the DLQ, not in the void.

ADR-005

Where Step Functions fits

  • Waiting for a human is a pending row, not a running workflow.
  • Step Functions coordinates bounded agent turns; SQS carries ingestion and delivery events.
